# Example Shield Health Basic
FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR DEMO ONLY. Not a real insurance policy.

## 1. Definitions
Hospital means an institution with at least 10 inpatient beds and round-the-clock qualified nursing staff, meeting the requirements in Section 5.2.
Waiting period means the time after policy start during which a claim is not payable.

## 2. Benefit table
| Benefit | Limit | Waiting period |
|---|---|---|
| Room rent | 5,000 per day | none |
| ICU charges | 10,000 per day | none |
| Cataract surgery | 50,000 per eye | 24 months |
| Maternity | 75,000 per delivery | 24 months |
| Ambulance | 2,000 per hospitalisation | none |

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Waiting periods
Pre-existing diseases are covered after 36 months of continuous cover.
An initial waiting period of 30 days applies to all illnesses, except accidents.

## 4. Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery is not covered unless it is needed after an accident.
Claims for self-inflicted injury are not payable.
Dental treatment is excluded unless it is due to an accident.
Treatment received outside India is not covered.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 5. Claims
### 5.1 Notification
Planned hospitalisation must be notified at least 48 hours before admission.
Emergency hospitalisation must be notified within 24 hours after admission.

### 5.2 Hospital requirements
A hospital under Section 1 must have an operation theatre and must keep daily records of patients.

## 6. Renewal
The grace period for renewal is 30 days. The free-look period is 15 days from receipt of the policy.
